## ⚔️ The Last Kingdom: Uhtred's Saga

**The Last Kingdom** is a *historical drama* television series based on Bernard Cornwell's series of novels, *The Saxon Stories*. It follows the adventures of **Uhtred of Bebbanburg**, a Saxon boy raised by Danes, as he struggles to reconcile his two identities and plays a key role in the birth of England.

***

### Reasons to Watch

I highly recommend this series for its compelling blend of history, action, and character development.

1.  **Gripping Storyline:** The conflict between the Saxons and the Danes is incredibly engaging.
2.  **Excellent Action:** The battles are brutal, realistic, and well-choreographed.
3.  **Complex Protagonist:** Uhtred's internal struggle with his identity is the heart of the show.
4.  **Historical Context:** It offers a fascinating look at the reign of King Alfred the Great.

***

### Key Characters

* **Uhtred of Bebbanburg:** The protagonist, a Saxon warrior raised by Vikings.
* **King Alfred the Great:** The King of Wessex who dreams of a united England.
* **Leofric:** Uhtred's loyal and witty friend (early seasons).
* **Finan:** A fierce Irish warrior who becomes one of Uhtred's closest companions.
